# Solar: credits

Code MIT. The worlds, the ship and everything else that is seen is made in code by Patch.

- three.js 0.186.1 (MIT).

## Music

Both pieces are by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/, text in `LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt`). They came through Patch's asset
library, which converted them from MP3 to Ogg Vorbis and added the credit to the Ogg comments; nothing else is changed.

- `music/dreams_become_real.ogg`: "Dreams Become Real" by Kevin MacLeod
  (https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1500027). Played on the title and in the menu.
- `music/floating_cities.ogg`: "Floating Cities" by Kevin MacLeod
  (https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1600018). Played where there is no air: in space
  and on the moon.

The menu's own sounds are made in code (`src/sound.js`).

## Sound

- Every sound is built in code (`src/sound.js`, MIT; its engine is Small Magic's): the wind, the engine, the room of
  the ship, the hatch, breathing on the moon, the knock of each step.
- Footsteps on grass, `sfx/step1.ogg` to `step5.ogg`: Kenney's Impact Sounds (kenney.nl), CC0 1.0.
- The full game also plays recordings from the Sonniss GDC Game Audio Bundles (sonniss.com), under their licence: free
  to use in a game, not to pass on as files. They are not part of this game's open licence, and are not in its repo:
  `MISSING_ASSETS.md` there names each one. The libraries: Coll Anderson (Gentle Wind), Discover Oregon (Wind and
  Storms; Wilderness Crickets), Hzandbits (Wind In Trees), Alexander Kopeikin (Quad ambience of Crimea in summer),
  Soundrangers (Foley Elements Foliage), Tovusound (Edward Foleyart Collection), PMSFX (STEPS Dirt & Gravel), Gregor
  Quendel (Designed Fire), Game Audio Factory (Dark Materials), 3maze (High Impact), Rock The Speakerbox (Broken).
