Cubemap capture
Cubemap pipeline: the faces the dome can see (5 of 6 at 180°; all 6 for the 360° and perspective views) are rendered from the rig position, then sampled once per output pixel. Auto matches cube texel density to output pixel density (×1.25).
Single-view pipeline: every vertex is projected straight into the fisheye in the vertex shader, so the scene is drawn once, at output resolution, with 4× MSAA. Dome master only; the other views use the cubemap. Best up to ~220° FOV.
